Dev48 minimum model inputs and executable source — 17 October 2026

The project software retains the root MIT License. The code contains only the
algorithm implementations and constructors/verifiers needed by the declared
16 cases. Original source identities are recorded in provenance; current
subset hashes are in EXECUTABLE_MANIFEST.json. New runs derive new path/hash
bindings, and do not inherit the old selected run's acceptance certificate.

The inputs/ tables are frozen modeled road networks, zone-access mappings,
aggregate model OD demand and time-graph construction settings. They are
engineering scenarios, not observed traveler records. They contain no raw
GTFS feed, full timetable itinerary collection, GPS trajectory, API key or
account credential. Source population/activity and timetable influences enter
through the already-frozen parent model demand. They are not measured traffic
or Census-published zone-level estimates.

Road-derived network data in the six new-city groups: © OpenStreetMap
contributors; ODbL 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . The actual bounded derivative
network tables used by the CG models are provided in inputs/ together with
the constructions needed to generate their expanded time graphs. The ODbL
database notice applies to those road-derived tables; independent solver code
retains its software license. Model demand and engineering parameter choices
are identified separately and are not portrayed as OSM observations.
