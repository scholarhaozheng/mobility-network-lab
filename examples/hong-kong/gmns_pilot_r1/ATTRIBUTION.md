# Data attribution and use

Road, turn, GTFS and detector source data: © Hong Kong SAR Government, Transport Department; provided through [DATA.GOV.HK](https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions). Census SSG/STPUG and 3D pedestrian source data: © Hong Kong SAR Government, Census and Statistics Department and Lands Department; provided through the [Common Spatial Data Infrastructure (CSDI) Portal](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/doc/TNC). This package contains compact derivatives, not provider archives. The providers retain intellectual-property rights. The model's demand is a hypothetical scenario, and the saved road data have unknown speed, lane count and capacity.

The private UrbanNav ground-truth text and its point-level derivatives are excluded. A reference trajectory exists in the private handoff, but no raw GNSS or general traffic trajectory is included here.
