# Rights and redistribution review

The [DATA.GOV.HK terms](https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions) permit download, reproduction and distribution with attribution to the Hong Kong Government, DATA.GOV.HK and the relevant provider. This applies to Transport Department road, GTFS and detector data used here. The [CSDI Portal terms](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/doc/TNC) similarly permit redistribution with attribution to the Government, the CSDI Portal and the Census and Statistics Department or Lands Department. The [CSDI FAQ](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/info/FAQ) specifies the CSDI attribution wording. All public derivatives carry these credits and retain a link to the terms. External data are not covered by the project's MIT software license.

The census ZIP files hosted on census2021.gov.hk were inspected but the model uses CSDI service attributes and polygons. The ZIPs are not copied to public candidates. The TCS report was linked but no aggregate table was taken. The [PolyU IPNL page](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/aae/ipn-lab/us/en/resources/urbannav-dataset/) labels UrbanNav as MIT, while the linked repository's license section does not present exact file license text. The small TST ground-truth text and point-level derivatives remain in the private handoff pending exact-file confirmation. Public candidates include only its aggregate QC counts and a source link. No ROS, LiDAR, camera, GNSS RINEX or credentials were downloaded.

The public candidate includes compact derived official road/zone/transit/detector tables and figures with the attribution above. The raw provider archives remain in the work handoff and are excluded from the public upload candidate. No file has been uploaded.
