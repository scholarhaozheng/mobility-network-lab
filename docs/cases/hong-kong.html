Read the evidence in model order

  1. GMNS objects, source register and rights boundary distinguish physical links, turn states, centroids, connectors and the two zone levels.
  2. Four-stage scenario gives the population/household/activity preparation, transferred trip rates, distribution, mode costs and observation grades.
  3. Static assignment gives FW, Algorithm B, independent conservation and turn checks under one BPR objective.
  4. Finite space–time CG, reference LP, Lagrangian and ADMM gate separates each mathematical certificate.
  5. Evidence contract names the assumptions, source rights and supported reproduction.
