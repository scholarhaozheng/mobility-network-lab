# Mobility Computation Lab

**City networks, travel demand and reproducible network computation.**

![Mobility Computation Lab cover using actual Central Boston roads and H3 zones; geography, not traffic values](assets/boston/visual_release_r1/mcl_boston_hero.png)

Cover geography: GMNS Plus 21_Boston (Apache-2.0), commit `116447ab641cca1ed34797d019c8e704063393c3`; H3 zones and composition: Mobility Computation Lab.

[City workflow](city-workflow.md) · [Network catalog](datasets.md) · [Visual results](visualizations.md) · [Open data](open-data.md) · [Quick start](getting-started.md) · [GMNS-compatible inputs](data-contract.md) · [Methods](methods.md)

The project is organized around a city network and its zones, demand and supporting observations. Its current computational route starts with prepared network tables; its separate OMDV tools support local metadata preparation. Acquisition, hierarchical zones, GPS matching and additional assignment methods are extensions, not capabilities implied by a catalog count.

Explore the [Central Boston five-map gallery](datasets/boston-central.md#boston-visual-gallery) and [current demand and transit feedback example](datasets/boston-behavior-feedback.md), browse the [road benchmark gallery](visualizations.md), run a [saved-result Boston query example](../examples/boston/SAVED_EXAMPLE.md) or a [reference solver example](getting-started.md), or [add a network](add-a-network.md). Supporting [open-data evidence](open-data.md) and [metadata tools](data-tools.md) remain available without becoming the main product definition.

The static `index.html` is generated by `tools/build_site.py`. The repository root `README.md` is the separate native GitHub homepage.

## Open mobility evidence for city studies

The supporting data section presents six source-qualified layers: the urban-centre frame, GTFS static, GTFS-Realtime, OSM map features, GBFS metadata and a model-interface crosswalk. Explore their definitions in the [evidence guide](open-data.md), then [process your own local records](data-tools.md). These research summaries and executable metadata tools complement the city workflow; they do not replace road networks, zone access, demand or assignment.
