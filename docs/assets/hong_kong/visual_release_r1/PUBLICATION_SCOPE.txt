# Hong Kong saved-position projection: narrow publication record

This record applies only to `hong_kong_reference_projection.png`, its SVG,
source sidecar, and the corresponding README/case-page embeds. It does not
change the disclosure decisions for original provider files, the complete
reference-position table, the complete saved-match table, or any other
UrbanNav-derived asset.

The PolyU IPNL [UrbanNav resource page](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/aae/ipn-lab/us/en/resources/urbannav-dataset/)
labels the UrbanNav **dataset** MIT. The same provider's
[dataset repository](https://github.com/IPNL-POLYU/UrbanNavDataset/blob/master/README.md#urbannav-hk-medium-urban-1)
lists `UrbanNav_TST_GT_raw.txt` as the ground-truth download for
UrbanNav-HK-Medium-Urban-1. The exact-file license wording is not reproduced
in that repository's License section. The project owner authorized publication
of this particular 12-point derived figure on 2026-10-02, relying on the
provider's dataset-level MIT designation and the exact-file dataset link.
This is a documented source-scope interpretation, not a claim that the project
owns or relicenses the provider's data. The historical R1 private handoff and
its rights review remain unchanged as records of that earlier decision.

The image shows 12 deterministic samples from the longest uninterrupted
segment of the frozen saved Viterbi match. Its location marks and lateral
offsets are derived from the provider's SPAN-CPT+IE reference positions and
the already-saved matched-link IDs and along-link distances. No matcher,
scientific solver, or observation acquisition was run to prepare the figure.
The public road geometry is the bounded official-derived Hong Kong GMNS
instance. Source identities, figure hashes and the selection rule are in
`hong_kong_reference_projection.source.json`.

Only the rendered PNG/SVG and non-coordinate sidecar are distributed. The raw
reference file, complete point and match CSVs, timestamps, provider archives,
GNSS RINEX, ROS, LiDAR, camera data and private solver states are not bundled.
The figure concerns one research vehicle; it is not observed OD, traffic
calibration, a representative traffic sample, or lane-level validation.

[doi:10.33012/navi.602](https://doi.org/10.33012/navi.602).
