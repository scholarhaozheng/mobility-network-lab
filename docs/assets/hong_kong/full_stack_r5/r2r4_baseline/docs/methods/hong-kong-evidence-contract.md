# Evidence and claim contract

`OFFICIAL_EXACT` and `OFFICIAL_SEGMENT_TRANSFER` describe posted-limit matching, not observed free speed. The model free speed, lane counts and capacities are `ENGINEERING_PROXY`. Census population is official but internal capture, attraction weighting, gravity impedance, mode constants, occupancy and PCE conversion are scenario assumptions. TCS coefficients represent all Hong Kong and are transferred to the pilot. Building storey-weighted area is not official gross floor area or employment.

Historical station AADT is not a one-hour directed link count. The detector record is a single snapshot outside the assignment period. The 2021 UrbanNav trace is a SPAN-CPT+IE ground-truth reference for one research vehicle, not raw GNSS or a representative traffic sample. Point-level derivatives remain private. The LP is a fixed-cost finite model; static FW/Algorithm B solve a BPR equilibrium model. CG and ADMM outputs are retained as exact gated transfer evidence.

Recheck the saved public bundle with `python verify_public_bundle.py --root <extracted-folder>` from a fresh extraction. No solver, raw archive or local prior-work tree is required for that audit.
