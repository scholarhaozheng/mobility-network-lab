Sioux Falls CG: original and added views

Compare Boston, Hong Kong and Sioux Falls in the existing atlas

The original Sioux cards are total/per-OD Phase I, a shared-capacity exchange, and a schematic map with ranked link flows. The added standalone Phase I, Phase II objective, and map-only views use the same historical run. Both 200 and 250 OD instances remain separate.

Historical independent full-DAG closure is unestablished. New shared-core results have their own aggregate full-graph certificates, shown separately; no missing per-OD pricing vector is invented. Native costs and original flow units are not silently converted to seconds, minutes, or PCE.

This historical inventory recorded the earlier OD scope, time model and result-version choices, including the total/per-OD Phase I composite, the Phase II objective with its signed reference gap, and the final movement map. Use the current cards linked above for the active presentation. This inventory counts view/result bindings, not experiments or PNG/SVG exports.
